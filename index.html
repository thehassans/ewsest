ABOUT EASTERN WORKFORCE SOLUTIONS

A huge majority of industries across Saudi Arabia—such as Oil & Gas, Petrochemicals, Heavy Construction, Energy, Logistics, Industrial Manufacturing, Marine, Infrastructure, and Facilities Management—are fundamentally dependent on skilled, reliable, and highly certified human capital. Across the Kingdom, workforce empowerment and strategic talent deployment are central contributors to the national economy and the realization of Saudi Vision 2030.

Eastern Workforce Solutions Establishment has continuously created sustainable value through its diversified, comprehensive portfolio of staffing and human capital management solutions. Leveraging extensive regional experience, rigorous vetting standards, and full compliance with Saudi labor laws—together with close client collaboration and industry-leading recruitment networks—Eastern Workforce Solutions delivers differentiated staffing solutions that meet demanding project timelines and strict HSE standards.

HSE & QUALITY

As an establishment, Eastern Workforce Solutions is uncompromisingly committed to the health, safety, and professional development of all our personnel. Through rigorous safety inductions, continuous skills training, and dedicated employee welfare programs tailored to client site specifications, we maintain zero-incident workplaces aligned with Aramco, SABIC, and international HSE standards.
